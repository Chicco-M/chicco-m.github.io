LazuNight Funkin' is a Friday Night Funkin' mod built on Psych Engine, made for LazuLight's first anniversary. It started as a three-person prototype and shipped six months later with twelve people on it — programmers, artists, and composers, all volunteers. I directed it.

The finished mod shipped four original tracks, two of them remixes of the talents' own songs, plus a two-phase playable character, custom noteskins and dialogue boxes, and original stage art and dialogue portraits. Small next to the big FNF mods, but each of those was something a specific person had to be assigned, scheduled, and unblocked on.

The turning point was the trailer. We cut one from the prototype and used it to approach the LazuLight Anniversary Web Development Team, which turned into an official partnership and, with it, the credibility to recruit the rest of the team.

Running a volunteer project taught me things a graded assignment can't. Nobody was obligated to be there, so scope had to stay small enough that people saw their work reach players, and the schedule had to survive contributors disappearing for a week without warning. We shipped on the date we announced, which I'm still proud of.

At launch the game reached 26,000+ views, 2,200+ downloads, and 600+ likes.
